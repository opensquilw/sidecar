Sidecar 車伴 · Privacy Policy 私隱政策

Last updated 最後更新: 2 October 2026

Your data stays on your phone

Sidecar has no accounts, no servers that store your data, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter (your cars, service records, reminders and photos of your documents) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and we never sell or share it.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a document, Sidecar asks for access to your camera or photo library so you can photograph or pick it. The image is saved on your device only.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Sidecar schedules notifications on your device for your renewal and service dates. Nothing is sent to us.

The help assistant

The 求助 assistant answers from guides built into the app and works offline. If an online assistant is ever switched on in a future version, the app will say so and ask before any message leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Use “Erase all data” in the app, or delete the app. Both remove everything Sidecar stored.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/opensquilw/sidecar.

你嘅資料只會留喺你部手機

車伴冇帳戶、冇伺服器儲存你嘅資料、冇分析追蹤、冇廣告。你輸入嘅所有嘢（車輛資料、保養記錄、提醒同文件相片）只會儲存喺你部裝置。我哋睇唔到，亦唔會出售或者分享。

相機同相簿

如果你選擇加入文件，車伴會要求使用相機或相簿，等你影低或者揀相。相片只會儲存喺你部裝置。

通知

如果你開啟提醒，車伴會喺你部裝置設定續牌同保養日期嘅通知，唔會傳送任何資料俾我哋。

求助助手

求助助手用 app 內置嘅指引回答，離線都用得。如果將來版本開啟網上助手，app 會先講明並徵求你同意，先會傳送訊息。

刪除資料

喺 app 入面用「清除所有資料」，或者刪除 app，就會移除車伴儲存嘅所有嘢。

聯絡

有問題可以喺 github.com/opensquilw/sidecar 開 issue。
